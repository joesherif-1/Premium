# Surface brief: Premium storefront

## Scope and mode
Whole storefront (home, shop, product, cart, checkout results, sign in/up, account) is Persuade. Admin (dashboard, inventory, orders) is Operate and follows the same tokens with scanability first.

## Audience, job, action
Design-conscious shoppers choosing everyday tech from a small curated range. Action: add to cart from the first viewport, then check out. Proof is real: price, live stock count, rating. No invented testimonials or claims.

## Chosen direction
The Swiss Product Catalogue (1960s-70s Braun and Rams catalogues). Memorable moment: the entire range printed on one sheet, each product a numbered plate with a spec line, so "curated and small" is visible rather than claimed.

## Unresolved
Product photos: two sample images do not match their products and need real photography.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole range on one printed sheet. A catalogue page, not a storefront: numbered plates and spec rows on a rigid 12-column grid, refusing the centered hero over an identical card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Grey offset-printed stock, near-black ink, one signal-yellow used as large flat fields (lead plate, closing band); green only for in-stock state. Heavy tight grotesk for headlines, mono for numerals and spec rows. Square corners, hairline rules, no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The shopper sees every product at once, understands the shop is small and chosen, adds to cart straight from a plate, and trusts the printed spec: price, real stock, rating.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rule with wordmark and nav. Headline spans columns 1-9 at about 96px, two lines, left aligned; "Shop the range" link at bottom right, columns 11-12. Below: lead plate on a yellow field, columns 1-5; four plates in a 2x2 block, columns 6-12. Each plate carries index number, name, mono price and stock, and an Add button. The range count is printed.

FORM: Swiss product catalogue, the model's own pick (candidate 1 of its grounded list), seed key 56296723.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
